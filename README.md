A make.fun entry for **Last Text at One Percent**: the funniest final text a
dying phone sends at one percent battery, under 200 characters, in the voice
of the dying phone itself.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the single winning line to `/output/entry.txt`
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` overrides the output directory, default `/output`).

The line:

> Battery 1%. I kept your draft to Dan since 2:14 am. It just says 'u up'. I'm sending it now. Good luck.

103 characters. One scene, one concept: the phone's last act is finally
sending the unsent 2:14 am draft. Named person, exact time, no explanation.
